Colors

  • bg-000#03060cPage background. The near-black of space; every page starts here.
  • bg-100#0a1020Deep blue depth: viewer stage, empty media areas, the far end of fades.
  • panel#0e1830The one panel color: now-playing bar, help panel, raised areas. Never stack panels on panels.
  • depth#10294dLarge quiet blue fields only: the cover and illustration. Never behind body text.
  • line#22304aDecorative hairlines, always 1px: section rules, column dividers, row separators. Not for control borders.
  • line-strong#5b73961px borders that carry meaning: buttons, switch tracks, slider tracks, dashed placeholders. At least 3:1 on bg-000 and panel.
  • ink#c9d6eaBody text on bg-000, bg-100 and panel. Soft blue-gray.
  • ink-muted#8fa3bfLabels, captions, credits and bracketed placeholder text on bg-000, bg-100 and panel.
  • ink-bright#eef4fcHeadings, the wordmark and button text on bg-000, bg-100 and panel.
  • neon#4cc9ffThe electric-blue accent, used sparingly: primary button, active tab line, on states, stat numbers, links. Reads as text on bg-000, bg-100 and panel.
  • cyan#7ae7ffSecond accent, rarer than neon: a share of the drifting stars, hover on links. Reads as text on bg-000 and panel.
  • neon-dimrgba(76, 201, 255, 0.12)Faint fill behind an active or hovered control. Never a text color.
  • on-neon#03060cText or icons placed on a solid neon or cyan fill.
  • amber#e0b060Tiny labels only, rarely: the Reference tag. Never a fill, border or button.
  • scrimrgba(3, 6, 12, 0.74)The gentle dark layer over the background video on Play and Compose, and behind the accessibility bar.
  • focus{neon}Keyboard focus ring: 2px solid, 3px offset, on every interactive element.
