The science behind the experience

NASA source collection

This draft adapts the glowing, flowing-strand interaction shown in your recording to Earth. The globe uses NASA Blue Marble and Black Marble imagery (from the open-source three-globe example set) embedded in this file. The strands, surface effects, Moon and music are creative interpretations, not live NASA measurements. The original NASA visuals below retain their scientific context. The music is generated from your controls, not extracted from NASA datasets.

Collection: NASA Earth Information Center. Original media needs an internet connection. The interactive scene and its music run locally.
