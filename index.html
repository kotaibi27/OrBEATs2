How to listen
Gentle harp-like arpeggios and soft chords follow the temperature. Warmer colors shift the whole harmony higher and slightly louder. The fixed temperature-to-root-pitch scale is the same for every region; the added musical intervals are artistic. Choose Single tone for the clearest direct comparison. No estimate means no new notes.

What this measures
The mean color-derived estimate of visible colored flow pixels in your box. It is not an area-weighted regional temperature, a measurement of current speed, or a live ocean reading. Red means warmer on this legend, not a danger alert; this visualization does not establish hazardous conditions.

How to interpret the sound: each tone represents a color category from your supplied legend. Loudness reflects relative color prominence in a small patch around the pointer.
