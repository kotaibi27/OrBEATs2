Audio check

This page opens the real Explore page below and plays each dataset through it, exactly as a visitor would. It listens for 3 seconds with an AnalyserNode and says whether sound was detected (a video that is quiet at that moment is tried again at two later points). Press one Play button, or check them all. Every Explore sound is Reem’s or NASA’s; the only other sound on the site is the Caldwell 73 music.
