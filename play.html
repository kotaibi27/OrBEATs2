How to play

Press Play game to hear a new random tune. Your own version then plays: change pitch, tempo, loudness and brightness until it sounds the same, and lock in. A soft background tone gets calmer as you get closer, so you can play by ear alone. Five rounds make a game.

Controls

Camera, keyboard and mouse, or touch: choose on the start screen, and switch at any time with “Change controls”.

Camera is off.

The camera picture is processed in your browser only. It is never recorded or uploaded.
